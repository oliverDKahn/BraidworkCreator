Drawing barriers

  • Click the left or right half of a diamond for a horizontal barrier; the top or bottom half for a vertical one.
  • The diagonal pair inside that diamond is erased automatically. Click the same half again to remove the barrier.
  • Hold and drag across diamonds to chain barriers together.
